Source: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

Last Updated: 2024

SECTION 1: BANK ACCOUNT OPENING AND KYC REQUIREMENTS
1. Officially Valid Documents (OVDs):
Under the RBI Master Direction on Know Your Customer (KYC), any of the following 6 documents serve as valid identity and address proof:
- Passport
- Driving License
- Proof of possession of Aadhaar number (Aadhaar card or e-Aadhaar)
- Voter's Identity Card issued by the Election Commission of India
- Job card issued by NREGA duly signed by an officer of the State Government
- Letter issued by the National Population Register containing details of name and address
Note: Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Form 60 is mandatory for financial verification.

2. Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA) / PMJDY:
- Zero balance requirement: No minimum balance is mandatory.
- No charge for cash deposits at bank branch or ATMs.
- Four free cash withdrawals per month at ATMs or bank counters.
- Free basic RuPay debit card.

3. Video KYC (V-CIP):
- Customers can open fully functional bank accounts without visiting a physical branch.
- Requirements: Original physical PAN card, Aadhaar number (authenticated via OTP), camera and microphone enabled smartphone/laptop, and geo-location within India.
- Bank official conducts live video interaction, captures photo and live signature on plain paper.

SECTION 2: UPI AND DIGITAL PAYMENT SAFETY PROTOCOLS
1. What is UPI?
Unified Payments Interface (UPI) developed by NPCI allows instant 24x7 inter-bank fund transfers via mobile phones using a Virtual Payment Address (VPA / UPI ID) or QR code.

2. Golden Safety Rules for UPI Users:
- UPI PIN is required ONLY to debit money from your account. You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN to RECEIVE money or receive refunds.
- If any caller or message asks you to enter your PIN or scan a QR code to 'claim cashback' or 'receive buyer payment', it is fraudulent.
- Never install remote screen-sharing software (such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, QuickSupport) on recommendation of unknown callers.
- Always check the beneficiary name displayed on screen before approving payment.
- Daily UPI limit is typically Rs. 1,00,000 for standard transactions (higher for capital markets/educational/hospital payments up to Rs. 5,00,000).

3. Cyber Crime Reporting:
- If money is fraudulently deducted, immediately dial '1930' (National Cyber Crime Helpline) or register complaint on cybercrime.gov.in within the golden hour (first 2-24 hours).
- Call your bank's 24x7 customer care number to freeze the account/block debit card/UPI ID.

SECTION 3: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL & RBI INTEGRATED OMBUDSMAN
1. Complaint Process:
- Step 1: Complain to the bank branch or via bank's online customer care portal. The bank must respond within 30 days.
- Step 2: If the bank rejects the complaint or fails to respond within 30 days, or if the customer is dissatisfied, escalate to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.
2. RBI Complaint Management System (CMS):
- Portal: https://cms.rbi.org.in
- Email: crpc@rbi.org.in | Postal: Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre, RBI, 4th Floor, Sector 17, Chandigarh - 160017.
- Toll-free Helpline: 14448.
- No fee is charged for filing complaints under the Ombudsman Scheme.
