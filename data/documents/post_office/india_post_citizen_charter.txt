Source: Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications, Government of India

Last Updated: 2024

SECTION 1: SPEED POST AND DOMESTIC PARCEL SERVICES
1. Speed Post:
Speed Post is the premium domestic courier service of India Post, offering fast and guaranteed delivery across all pin codes in India.
- Weight Limit: Up to 35 kilograms.
- Delivery Standards: Local delivery within 1-2 days; metro-to-metro within 1-3 days; rest of India within 2-5 days depending on location.
- Tracking: Every Speed Post item receives a unique 13-character tracking number (format: two letters, 9 digits, 'IN', e.g., SP123456789IN).
- SMS Tracking: Send SMS 'POST TRACK <13-digit Consignment ID>' to 166 or 51969.
- Compensation: In case of delay, compensation equals the Speed Post fee paid. In case of loss or damage, compensation is double the Speed Post fee or Rs. 1000, whichever is less.

2. Domestic Parcel Booking:
- Standard Registered Parcel: Ideal for heavier goods up to 20 kg.
- Packaging Requirements: Parcels must be packed securely in strong boxes, cardboard, or cloth wrap. Liquid, perishable, corrosive, or flammable goods are prohibited.
- Declaration: Parcels sent for interstate movement with commercial value may require an e-way bill / GST declaration under prevailing tax laws.
- Address Format: Clearly print the sender's name, complete address, mobile number, and PIN code on the top-left corner. Print the addressee's full name, address, landmark, PIN code, and mobile number in the center.

SECTION 2: POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (POSB) ACCOUNTS
1. Post Office Savings Account (POSA):
- Eligibility: Resident individual Indian citizens. Can be single, joint (up to 3 adults), or minor operated through guardian (minors aged 10+ can operate individually).
- Minimum Opening Amount: Rs. 500 in cash or cheque.
- Minimum Balance Requirement: Rs. 500. If minimum balance is not maintained at financial year end, a maintenance fee is deducted.
- Interest Rate: 4.0% per annum credited annually.
- Features: ATM Card facility, Cheque book facility, e-Banking / m-Banking, and Aadhaar seeding for government DBT subsidies.
- Nomination: Mandatory at the time of opening the account.

2. Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS):
- Eligibility: Individuals aged 60 years or above, or retired civilian employees aged 55-60 years with retirement benefits.
- Maximum Deposit: Rs. 30 Lakhs.
- Tenure: 5 years (extendable by 3 years).
- Interest: Paid quarterly directly into the investor's savings account.
- Tax Benefit: Eligible for Section 80C deduction.

SECTION 3: INDIA POST PAYMENTS BANK (IPPB)
- Overview: IPPB is a 100% Government of India owned payments bank under the Department of Posts.
- Doorstep Banking: Postmen and Gramin Dak Sevaks (GDS) visit customers' residences with handheld biometric micro-ATM devices.
- Account Opening: Paperless, 100% digital account opening in under 5 minutes using Aadhaar and biometric fingerprint/iris scan. No paper copies required.
- Minimum Balance: Zero balance account without penalty.
- Services Offered: Domestic money transfers, utility bill payments, DBT subsidy receipt, life and health insurance distribution, and Post Office Savings scheme deposits.
- Customer Helpline: 155299 (Toll-Free).
