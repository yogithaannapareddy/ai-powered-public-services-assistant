SECTION 1: STATE CITIZEN CERTIFICATES (SERVICEPLUS & STATE PORTALS)
1. Income Certificate:
- Purpose: Required for availing educational scholarships, fee reimbursements, welfare schemes, and reservation benefits (e.g., EWS quota).
- Issuing Authority: Revenue Department (Tahsildar / Sub-Divisional Magistrate).
- Validity: Generally valid for one financial year.
- Required Documents: Salary slip / Form 16 (for salaried persons), income tax return or income affidavit on stamp paper (for self-employed/business), Ration card / Food security card, Aadhaar card, electricity bill, and passport size photograph.
- Delivery Timeline: Standard 7 to 15 working days through online citizen service portal (MeeSeva, e-District, Nadakacheri, Aaple Sarkar, etc.).

2. Community / Caste Certificate:
- Purpose: To establish scheduled caste (SC), scheduled tribe (ST), or other backward class (OBC) status for statutory reservations in education and government jobs.
- Documents Required: Proof of caste (father's or family member's caste certificate), School Leaving Certificate / Transfer Certificate (TC) showing caste, Aadhaar card, residence proof, self-declaration affidavit.
- Validity: SC/ST certificates are permanent. OBC non-creamy layer (NCL) certificates are valid for 1 financial year.

3. Domicile / Residence Certificate:
- Purpose: Proof that an individual is a permanent resident of a specific state/union territory for state job quotas and state quota college admissions.
- Proof of Continuous Residence: School marksheets showing minimum required years of study (usually 3 to 7 years in that state), property tax receipt, electricity bills, or voter ID card.

SECTION 2: DIGILOCKER DIGITAL WALLET PLATFORM
1. What is DigiLocker?
- Cloud storage platform providing citizen digital access to authentic documents issued by government departments, educational boards, and statutory bodies.
- Capacity: 1 GB cloud storage for personal uploaded documents; unlimited storage for digitally issued documents from registered issuers.

2. Legal Status and Validity:
- Legally recognized under Rule 9A of Information Technology Rules, 2016.
- Documents in DigiLocker issued by authorities (Aadhaar, Driving License, Vehicle RC, Insurance Policy, Class X & XII marksheets, University degrees) are deemed equivalent to original physical documents.
- Traffic police and transport authorities cannot insist on physical copies if digital DL/RC is produced via DigiLocker or mParivahan.

3. Step-by-Step Issuance in DigiLocker:
- Step 1: Sign up using Aadhaar number and verify mobile OTP.
- Step 2: Set a personal 6-digit security PIN.
- Step 3: Click 'Search Documents' and search for your institution (e.g., State Board, CBSE, UIDAI, MoRTH).
- Step 4: Enter specific identifier (Registration number, Roll number, or Chassis/Vehicle number).
- Step 5: DigiLocker fetches the cryptographically signed electronic certificate and saves it in 'Issued Documents' section.
